# MobLog 1.21

- Share personal loot sources, creature records and encounter locations, and ore, herb, and fishing spots directly with another MobLog player.
- Add **Send finds**, **Transfers**, and **Imported finds** to **Share finds**, available from the main window and with `/ml share`. Share a selected item from the item catalog or a personal gathering spot with Alt + right-click on the world map.
- Use first and last names for Forever recipients and character names or **Name-Realm** for Retail. Show the sender, record count, progress, and cancellation controls.
- Preview new records before importing, choose categories or individual entries, and skip already known records. Review imported item sources, creature coordinates, and gathering spots in the corresponding views.
- Display imported gathering spots on the world map and minimap with a MobLog setting for their visibility. Keep personal statistics separate.
- Remove individual imported records or undo an entire package. Show a transfer-time notice for selections above 1,000 finds.
