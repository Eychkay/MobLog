# MobLog 1.1.9

- Show recorded item totals for every character with stock, broken down by bags, equipped gear, and personal bank tabs. Show shared bank stock separately, with recorded tab names and quantities.
- Enable inventory details on item tooltips by default. Keep the toggle under **Items → My inventory**.
- Draw saved minimap gathering spots as hollow rings with transparent centers and small material icons in the upper-right corner.
- Use the recorded ore or herb icon on the world map and minimap when MobLog can identify the material from gathering loot. Known stone byproducts no longer stand in for the ore.
- Refresh the map markers when new gathering loot identifies the recorded material. Older spots can use their existing loot records.
