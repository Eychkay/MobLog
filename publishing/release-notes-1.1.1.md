# MobLog 1.1.1 — Background Preparation & Clearer Locations

- Prepare the catalog in the background after login and continue manually started
  item scans with the window closed. Both options are enabled by default, slower
  while hidden, and paused in combat.
- See personal encounter areas separately from known habitats, and filter Mobs
  and Items by All locations, Your encounters or Known habitats.
- Open supported dungeon maps without exact coordinates and enjoy fixes for
  missing model previews and zoomed-in right-drag panning.
- Get clearer item-index status, bounded retries and an explicit unresolved count
  instead of a misleading complete state.
- Reduce duplicate catalog tables and unnecessary background grouping work.

Install both **MobLog** and **MobLog_TrinityDB** from MobLog-1.1.1.zip. Your records
and settings are preserved; no extra addon is required. Restart WoW after updating.
Dungeon tracking remains loot-based; open-world death detection is unchanged.
Initial Lua file loading can still briefly pause the client.
