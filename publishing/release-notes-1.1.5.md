# MobLog 1.1.5 — Discoveries

Open the new **Discoveries** tab to track your personal bestiary milestones, rare and elite encounters, and recorded kills. The journal shares the album's parchment look and lets you filter by category and completion. New seals can record the character and NPC involved, and existing personal records also count.

- **Retail / Midnight:** Extract both folders from `MobLog-1.1.5.zip`.
- **Forever 1.60.1:** Extract both folders from `MobLog-1.1.5-Forever.zip`.

Use your client's `Interface/AddOns` folder, restart WoW, and open MobLog with `/moblog`. Existing records and preferences are retained. Distinct-NPC goals use each client's catalog as a balancing guide. The Forever reference catalog is incomplete.
