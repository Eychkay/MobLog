# MobLog 1.19 · Fishing spots and inventory tooltips

Revisit your favorite fishing spots on the world map and minimap. MobLog uses your character's approximate position when fishing loot appears. Hover over a marker to see the number of recorded catches and how much of each item you found there. Select an item in the catalog and open **Gathering sources** to find its recorded spots.

Mining, herb, and fishing markers use hollow rings on the minimap, leaving room for WoW's live tracking dots. When MobLog can identify the material or catch, its icon appears in the upper-right corner of the ring and on the world-map marker.

Hover over an item to see which characters have it and how much is in bags, equipped, or on each recorded bank tab. The shared bank appears once. Log in on each character and open their bank to record its contents. Toggle the breakdown under **Items → My inventory**.

## Install for your client

- **Retail** uses `MobLog-1.19.zip`. Extract `MobLog` and `MobLog_TrinityDB` into `_retail_/Interface/AddOns/`.
- **Forever** uses `MobLog-1.19-Forever.zip`. Extract `MobLog` and `MobLog_ForeverDB` into `_forever_/Interface/AddOns/`.

Fully restart WoW after updating. Your existing records and settings stay with their client.
