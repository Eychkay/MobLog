# MobLog 1.1.9 · Inventory tooltips and gathering markers

Hover over an item to find out who has it. MobLog shows the recorded total for each character and how many are in bags, equipped, or on each recorded bank tab. The shared bank appears once. Log in on each character and open their bank so MobLog can record what is there.

Saved mining and herbalism spots appear as rings on the minimap with a transparent center for WoW's live tracking dots. An identified material, such as Tin Ore, gets a small icon on the ring and a matching icon on the world map. Spots without an identified material use a plain ring and a world-map profession icon.

## Install for your client

- **Retail** uses `MobLog-1.1.9.zip`. Extract `MobLog` and `MobLog_TrinityDB` into `_retail_/Interface/AddOns/`.
- **Forever** uses `MobLog-1.1.9-Forever.zip`. Extract `MobLog` and `MobLog_ForeverDB` into `_forever_/Interface/AddOns/`.

Fully restart WoW after updating. Your existing records and preferences stay with their client. The inventory tooltip display is on by default and can be toggled under **Items → My inventory**.
