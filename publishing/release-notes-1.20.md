# MobLog 1.20 · Inventory faction filters

Find items on characters of your own faction by default in **Items → My inventory**. Choose **All factions** to include the rest of your recorded characters. Names use class colors, and characters from the opposing faction are labeled.

Item tooltips show all factions by default. To restrict them, open **Options → AddOns → MobLog → Inventory factions in item tooltips** and choose **Own faction**. This setting is separate from the inventory filter. Shared storage is included in both modes.

Older records may show **Faction unknown** under **All factions**. Log in once on each of those characters to record their faction.

## Install for your client

- **Retail** uses `MobLog-1.20.zip`. Extract `MobLog` and `MobLog_TrinityDB` into `_retail_/Interface/AddOns/`.
- **Forever** uses `MobLog-1.20-Forever.zip`. Extract `MobLog` and `MobLog_ForeverDB` into `_forever_/Interface/AddOns/`.

Fully restart WoW after updating. Your existing records and settings stay with their client.
