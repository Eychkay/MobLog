# MobLog 1.1.4 — Open your bestiary

Browse your collection as a parchment album and open each creature on a double-page spread. Explore its 3D model, personal loot history, characters and locations without leaving the book.

Known names remain visible on undiscovered cards. Filter the album by Hostile, Non-hostile, Undiscovered or Unknown attitude, and combine that choice with your existing search and collection filters. Previous and Next move through the filtered album.

Prefer the list? Its model preview is now larger, with Details & Places below and a More details button for opening the full creature page. Back to list restores your previous position. The book also has a larger model area, a compact header and a closer default zoom.

## Download the correct package

- **Retail / Midnight:** `MobLog-1.1.4.zip` → `MobLog` + `MobLog_TrinityDB`.
- **Forever 1.60.1:** `MobLog-1.1.4-Forever.zip` → `MobLog` + `MobLog_ForeverDB`.

Extract both folders from the matching package into your client's `Interface/AddOns` folder. Restart the game if installing new files, then open MobLog with `/moblog`, `/ml` or its minimap button. Existing records and preferences are retained.

Inside dungeons and raids, recording remains loot-based. Missing available details are recovered after leaving. Catalog coverage and automatic tracking depend on the information available for the selected game client.
