# MobLog 1.19

- Record fishing spots when the game reports fishing loot. Nearby catches share a location, with catch counts and quantities for each item.
- Show fishing spots on the world map and minimap. Look up the recorded locations of caught items under **Items → Item catalog → Gathering sources**.
- Draw saved gathering spots as hollow minimap rings so WoW's live tracking dots can show through. Fishing rings are turquoise.
- Show the recorded material or catch icon on the world map and in the corner of its minimap ring. Update both markers when new loot identifies the item. Known stone byproducts no longer replace ore icons.
- Show item totals for each character with recorded stock, broken down by bags, equipped gear, and personal bank tabs. Show the shared bank separately with its recorded tab names and quantities.
- Enable the inventory breakdown on item tooltips by default. Toggle it under **Items → My inventory**.
