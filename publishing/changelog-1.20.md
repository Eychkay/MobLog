# MobLog 1.20

- Show class-colored character names in inventory details and character filters. Mark characters from the opposing faction in the inventory and item tooltips.
- Add **Own faction** and **All factions** to **Items → My inventory**. The default is **Own faction**, and the selected scope applies to item counts and storage locations.
- Add a separate faction setting for item tooltips under **Options → AddOns → MobLog**. Tooltips default to **All factions** and calculate their totals from the selected scope.
- Keep shared storage available in both modes. Older characters with no faction on record appear as **Faction unknown** under **All factions** until they log in.
