# MobLog 1.1.7 — My inventory

Open **Items → My inventory** to see what your characters own and where they left it. Search by name or item ID, filter by character or item type, and scroll through the recorded locations. Click the coin balance for a breakdown by character and shared bank. You can enable inventory counts on regular item tooltips from the same tab.

Log in on each character to record their bags, equipped gear, and carried money. Open their bank to record its contents. Shared storage is included once when the client makes it available. The displayed totals reflect the last recorded balances and holdings.

- **Retail:** Extract `MobLog` and `MobLog_TrinityDB` from `MobLog-1.1.7.zip`.
- **Forever:** Extract `MobLog` and `MobLog_ForeverDB` from `MobLog-1.1.7-Forever.zip`.

Install both folders into the matching client's `Interface/AddOns` directory, then restart WoW or reload the UI. Existing records and preferences are retained.
